_As part of my ongoing restoration of maker.rip, I'm writing up the projects from the old version of this site that never got a post of their own._

Between getting the [Ooznest WorkBee](https://magazine.raspberrypi.com/hackspace/issues/40) in the summer of 2020 and the start of 2021, I cut a lot of things on it. Most of them were gifts, a few were commissions, and some were an attempt at a business called Monumental Me: personalised displays for the home and office, where a coaching session helped you work out what the words should be, and then I carved them. The old site had a portfolio page for each one, which is a bit much for what amounted to a photo and a paragraph, so here they all are in one place. I've already written up [how one of them came together](/2021/02/22/from-idea-to-fruition/) if you want the process.

## COHO vision statement

![The finished hexagonal oak COHO display, with the logo and vision statement in black, held up in the COHO office](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-coho.jpg)

An early customer for Monumental Me, this vision statement display hangs in the offices of COHO and serves as a reminder of the company's vision and why it exists. It was also my first go at filling a carving with epoxy, which [didn't go entirely to plan](https://www.instagram.com/p/CEFLTg0DyOT/) the first time round. The customer was kind enough to say:

> We're thrilled to have an incredibly beautiful, bespoke piece of art hanging proudly in the office, reminding the whole team of what we need to do.

## T. S. Eliot quote display

![A pine board carved with three lines from The Love Song of J. Alfred Prufrock in script, with large black quotation marks either side](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-ts-eliot.jpg)

Becky reads a lot of books (and I mean a lot), which means she has many (many) favourite quotes. So when I approached her with the offer of a quote display, we knew trying to whittle it down to a select one was going to be tough. We used the coaching consultation part of the process to discuss each of her shortlist, using it to understand what each one means to her, and before we knew it this offering by T. S. Eliot was the obvious choice, as it inspires her to be herself.

![Becky holding the finished quote display in front of her, smiling](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-ts-eliot-becky.jpg)

## Be · Do · Have display

![A square MDF panel carved with the words be, do and have in script, each followed by three lines of text](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-be-do-have-mdf.jpg)

The Be · Do · Have process is a way of understanding what you would like to _have_, and works backwards from there, first to understand what you need to _do_ to have those things, before landing on who you need to _be_ to do them. The MDF test cut above had all three. But this one was for Caroline, who is already pretty content with what she has, and already knows what she did to get it, so the finished board only needed the one.

![Caroline holding the finished display, a dark-stained pine board carved with the word be in script above three lines of text](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-be-do-have.jpg)

## "Soviet" clock

![A wall clock made from interlocking blocks of oak with square cut-outs, with black hands, hanging on a grey wall](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-soviet-clock.jpg)

Although inspired by clock designs I found on Pinterest, this particular design is 100% my own. Whilst unintentional, more than one person has mentioned that it looks very Soviet in style, which I put down to the choice of font. It was originally intended as a Christmas gift, but a small error during the build resulted in a [snapped endmill](https://www.instagram.com/p/CGfDA8ujQm_/) and a scorched notch in the final piece, so I finished it by hand and kept it for myself.

![The clock on the wall above a sofa in the corner of the living room, beside a floor lamp and three framed prints](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-soviet-clock-2.jpg)

## The cats have been fed

![A pine board with The Cats Have Been Fed, Don't Listen To Their Bullshit painted in blue, with cat ears and whiskers on the first word](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-cats-have-been-fed.jpg)

Based on a design by ChameleonCuttablesUS, this was a commission from my good friend Emma, who wanted it as a gift for someone else. It proved popular enough that within a couple of hours of posting it on Instagram I already had an order for another one from one of my followers.

## Cribbage board

![A live-edge yew cribbage board with rows of peg holes, photographed on a dark textured surface](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-cribbage-board.jpg)

A cribbage board crafted out of yew, given to my father for Christmas. I've got memories of him playing cribbage while on various holidays in France, so I'd always wanted to make a board for him.

## "We're All Mad Here"

![A beech board carved with We're All Mad Here and a wide Cheshire Cat grin, highlighted in silver, on a blue background](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-were-all-mad-here.jpg)

A Christmas gift for our friend Amber, a massive fan of Alice in Wonderland, crafted from beech and finished with silver spray paint.

## Abstract cat wall display

![A square oak board carved with a flowing abstract cat outline, highlighted in silver, held up against a blue background](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-abstract-cat.jpg)

Perfect for any cat lover that spurns the cutesy cat aesthetic, preferring something more modern and abstract. I made this 190×190mm display with the intention of selling it on Etsy. Highlighted in silver, I think the bold design really helps it stand out.

## Bunny with bow tie and glasses

![A square oak board carved with a rabbit wearing glasses and a bow tie, held up against a blue background](/assets/img/2026/10/cnc-bunny-bow-tie.jpg)

Perfect for any bunny lover with a touch of class and sophistication. Another 190×190mm display built with Etsy in mind, this time highlighted in caffè espresso chalk paint, where the subtle contrast does the work.
