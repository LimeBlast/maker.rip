_As part of my ongoing restoration of maker.rip, I've gathered up the short pages the old version of this site had for each review I wrote for HackSpace magazine._

Every issue of HackSpace is free to download as a PDF, and the magazine is published under a Creative Commons licence, so here are the reviews as they appeared in print. Click a page to read it full size.
