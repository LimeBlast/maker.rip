## The idea

In February 2020 I started an Instagram account for Monumental Me, "my idea for helping people achieve their monumental goals". The pitch was personalised displays for the home and office, hand carved from wood, with a coaching session first to work out what the words should be. That same month I went to Birmingham for a two-day introduction to coaching course with The Coaching Academy.
