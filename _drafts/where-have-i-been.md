Like the zombies this site is helping me prepare for, this site has returned from the dead. But first, a quick history lesson:

- I originally started this site in 2016 with the ambition of completing all the projects in _The Maker's Guide to the Zombie Apocalypse_ book
- I made [13 posts](/categories/the-makers-guide-to-the-zombie-apocalypse/) on this topic, completing 7 of the 20 projects contained in the book
- Following this I expanded the scope of the content and started working on other projects based around Arduino and Raspberry Pi
- In 2017 I got a 3D printer.
- In 2018 I started learning woodworking skills.
- At some point mid-2019 I swapped away from a Jekyll-hosted site to a Ghost blog.
